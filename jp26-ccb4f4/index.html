Key Learnings

  1. Japan ≠ Europe for hiking logistics — no wild camping, structured systems, efficiency > freedom
  2. Tateyama is a designed experience — must follow transport system, not flexible like road-trip mountains
  3. Fuji = perfect complement — easy, flexible, scenic, car-friendly
  4. Mixed camping is optimal — alpine lightweight at Tateyama, comfortable car camping at Fuji
  5. Japan is cheaper than Europe — lower accommodation, food, transport vs Dolomites/Switzerland
